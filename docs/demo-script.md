# Market Pulse — 2:40–3:00 Demo Script

Setup before recording: run `docker compose up --build`, wait for all three
services to report healthy, and log out of any existing session so the login
screen is the first thing on camera. Have the demo credentials
(`demo@growwpulse.app` / `Demo@12345`) ready to paste.

---

### 0:00–0:15 — The problem with ordinary watchlists

> "Every stock watchlist I've used answers the same question: what's the
> price right now? But that's not actually what I want to know when I open
> the app. What I want to know is — what changed since the last time I
> looked, and does it actually matter?"

### 0:15–0:35 — Introduce Market Pulse

> "This is Market Pulse. Instead of a table of numbers, it remembers your
> last visit, compares it to right now, and runs every stock through a
> deterministic, explainable scoring engine — price movement, volume
> anomalies, benchmark divergence, and volatility — to decide what actually
> deserves your attention."

*(Log in with the demo account.)*

### 0:35–1:10 — Show "You Were Away"

> "The moment I log in, I see this: I was away for [X], and out of my ten
> stocks, [N] changed, and [M] of those actually deserve my attention. Not
> a wall of tickers — a summary of what's different."

*(Point at the "Top changes" cards; note the "did not meaningfully change"
section collapsing the noise.)*

### 1:10–1:35 — Open a meaningful stock change

> "Let's look at Reliance — it's flagged HIGH attention. I'll click into it."

*(Click the Reliance card or radar node.)*

### 1:35–1:55 — Show Change Story and explanation

> "This is the Change Story. Attention score: [score] out of 100. Previous
> price, current price, and — critically — *why* it was flagged: the price
> move crossed my threshold, volume was elevated versus the average, and it
> diverged from the benchmark. Every number here traces back to a rule, not
> a guess."

*(Scroll to show the score breakdown bars and the timeline.)*

### 1:55–2:20 — Show Market Radar

> "Zooming out — this is the Market Radar. Every stock in my watchlist is a
> node in an attention field. The ones that matter pull toward the center
> and pulse. The quiet ones just sit at the edge. It's a completely
> different way to scan a watchlist than a table."

### 2:20–2:40 — Click "Simulate Market Update"

> "To show this isn't static — I'll simulate a new market tick."

*(Click "Simulate Market Update.")*

### 2:40–3:00 — Show the new change and architecture

> "And immediately, a new change appears on the radar and in the feed —
> generated by the same deterministic engine, not scripted for the demo.
> Under the hood this is a FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend with an
> append-only snapshot ledger, a pluggable market-data-provider interface —
> today it's a mock, tomorrow it could be a real feed — and a React
> frontend that never trusts the client for state. That's Market Pulse."
